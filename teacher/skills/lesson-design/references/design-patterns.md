# Lesson design patterns

Pick a structure by **what the students should be able to do at the end**, not by which pattern is
currently fashionable. The pattern names below are yours; never put them in front of the teacher.

## Choosing a structure

| The primary goal is… | Use | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| understand a mechanism or cause | **observe → infer → name** | students meet a phenomenon first, describe what they notice, and only then get the vocabulary |
| explain something in the world | **instance → rule → new instance** | one worked case, the generalisation, then a fresh case they handle alone |
| handle exam questions | **worked → guided → independent** | a solved example with the reasoning shown, then one done together, then one alone |
| resolve a real problem | **problem → constraints → solution → critique** | the situation and its constraints up front; the solution is judged against them |
| remember and use facts | **retrieve → check → space** | short recall attempts before re-teaching, then revisit later in the lesson |

If the brief's `activity_level` is `high`, the students should be doing something in the first five
minutes. If it is `low`, do not fake activity — a clear explanation that lands beats a rushed group
task that does not.

## Time budget

| Lesson length | Teacher talk | Student work | Wrap-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| 40–45 min | ≤ 20 min, in at most 3 blocks | ≥ 18 min | 5 min |
| 90 min | ≤ 35 min | ≥ 45 min | 10 min |

Never write a plan whose blocks sum to more than `duration_minutes`. If the goals do not fit, say
which goal to drop rather than silently shortening everything.

## The questions that carry a lesson

Two or three, no more. A good carrying question:

- can be answered in the students' current vocabulary,
- has a wrong-but-reasonable answer worth surfacing,
- is the thing the lesson's structure exists to resolve.

Write them the way a teacher says them aloud. 「为什么白天太阳照着还是觉得冷？」 beats
「请分析大气受热过程的影响因素」.

## Where students get lost

Name the two or three specific places for this topic, and give the teacher a move for each. Generic
advice ("注意难点") is a defect. Concrete: 「学生常把『太阳辐射』和『地面辐射』的作用顺序弄反——让他们先
标出热量从哪来到哪去，再讲名称。」

## Self-check before handing over

1. Every slot in the brief is either used or named as an assumption. Nothing silently dropped.
2. The minutes add up to `duration_minutes`.
3. The primary goal is reachable by the end; each secondary goal has a moment in the sequence.
4. At least one place lets a student be wrong in a way the lesson uses.
5. No framework name appears in anything the teacher reads.
6. Two to three objectives, not four or more.
7. Every default you filled is listed under 我替你定的事.
8. Nothing in the plan asks the teacher to make a technical decision.
