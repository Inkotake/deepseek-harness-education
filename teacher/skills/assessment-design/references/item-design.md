# Item design

## Rules that make a question usable

1. **One thing per question.** A question that tests the concept and the calculation and the
   vocabulary marks all three, and the teacher cannot tell which one the student missed.
2. **Answerable from the stated range.** If the question needs a fact the paper does not supply and
   the range does not cover, the question is unfair, not hard.
3. **No clue leakage.** Watch for the grammatical agreement between the stem and the key, an
   obviously longest correct option, or two distractors that mean the same thing.
4. **Distractors are real mistakes.** Every wrong option should be what a student who made a specific
   error would choose. "Obviously wrong" options waste the question.
5. **The stem carries the situation, the options carry only the choice.** Do not repeat the whole
   scenario in all four options.
6. **Units and precision stated.** A numeric answer without its unit or its rounding rule is not a
   complete question.

## Difficulty bands

| Band | Share | What it asks for |
|---|---|---|
| 基础 | ~60% | direct recall or a single application of one idea |
| 中等 | ~30% | two ideas combined, or a familiar idea in an unfamiliar context |
| 挑战 | ~10% | transfer, or a judgement the students must justify |

Assign the band **after** writing the question, by asking what a student must do to answer it. Do
not label a question 挑战 because it is longer.

## Mark allocation

- Allocate marks to the **steps a student must get right**, not to how long the question looks.
- A multi-step question's marks must be splittable, so partial credit means something: state which
  step earns which marks.
- Never let a single question carry more than about a quarter of the total; one bad question then
  cannot decide the paper.
- The marks must sum exactly to the total. Add them twice.

## Time budget

Rough reading-and-answering rates, for a check that the paper fits:

| Question type | Approximate time per question |
|---|---|
| 选择题 (single answer) | 40–60 s |
| 填空题 | 60–90 s |
| 简答 / 计算 (multi-step) | 4–8 min |

Add up the total and compare with the stated duration. If it exceeds the budget, cut questions
rather than shortening every question into vagueness.

## Self-check before handing over

1. Marks sum exactly to the total, checked twice.
2. Every range item appears in the coverage table; nothing is tested twice.
3. The difficulty bands match the stated ratio.
4. Every question has an answer, a mark allocation, and a partial-credit rule.
5. Every numeric answer states its unit.
6. No question depends on a fact outside the range or outside what the paper supplies.
7. No distractor is obviously wrong; no key is grammatically identifiable.
8. The time budget adds up to the stated duration.
9. The 「本次试卷的设定」 block is present and consistent with the paper that follows it.
